TanyaIT

A helpdesk assistant built from three years of running IT support. Paste a complaint the way office staff actually write it ("internet lemot", "printer ngadat") and TanyaIT classifies the category, sets a priority with an SLA, finds the matching fix in a 27-article knowledge base and drafts the reply. It learns from corrections.

Tickets, knowledge base and replies are in Indonesian, because that is how users in my workplace report problems. Models: multinomial Naive Bayes plus TF-IDF retrieval with an Indonesian tokenizer and slang dictionary, all in the browser.

Model quality

5-fold cross-validation on the labelled tickets. Category blends Naive Bayes (60%) with the best knowledge-base match (40%). Priority adjusts the Naive Bayes estimate with business-impact signals such as "semua cabang", "kasir" or "tebusan".
